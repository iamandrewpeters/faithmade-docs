# Site Settings: Branding & Design

**Site Settings** is your design control center — colors, fonts, menu styling, buttons, and more, all with a live preview of your actual homepage right next to the controls.

Go to **Site → Site Settings** in your dashboard (or **Design → Site Settings**) to open it.

:::tip Site Settings vs. the Customizer
If you've used FaithMade for a while, you may remember doing this in the WordPress **Customizer**. Site Settings is the newer, faster replacement — same underlying settings, but with instant color updates and a cleaner layout. A few dashboard shortcuts may still say "Customize" and drop you into the old Customizer; that still works, but Site Settings is where you should make changes going forward.
:::

![Site Settings open to the Branding tab, with the live preview of the homepage on the right](/img/site-settings/branding-live-preview.png)

## How It Works

The panel on the left has your settings, grouped into tabs. The preview on the right shows your actual homepage. **Color changes apply instantly** as you type or pick a swatch — no saving required to see them. Changes that affect layout (fonts, some toggles) reload the preview after you save.

## What's in Each Tab

**Branding** — Your core brand colors (Accent, Accent Hover, Light, Dark, and a few supporting tones), plus your logo and default images used across sermons, series, groups, and events when a specific item doesn't have its own image.

**Typography** — Body and heading fonts, font weights, and text colors. Toggle "Customize Body Font" off to fall back to your theme's default.

![Typography tab with font and color controls](/img/site-settings/typography.png)

**Menu** — Colors and hover effects for your header navigation: top-level links, submenu dropdowns, and the mobile/fullscreen menu.

![Menu tab with header navigation color controls](/img/site-settings/menu.png)

**Buttons** — Primary, Secondary, and Tertiary button color sets, border/shadow styles, and whether buttons "lift" on hover.

**Giving** — The simple giving link/embed shown on your site (a giving URL or embed code, text-to-give number, and app store giving links). If your church uses the full [Giving](../giving/setting-up-giving) plugin for online donations, that's configured separately — this tab is for the basic giving info shown in your header/footer.

**Top Bar** — The announcement bar that runs across the very top of your site: message text and a call-to-action button.

**Bubble** — The floating popup in the bottom corner of every page, usually used to highlight next steps or upcoming events.

![Bubble tab showing Show Bubble, Trigger Icon, and Use Connect Flows controls](/img/site-settings/bubble.png)

Turn it on or off with **Show Bubble**, pick a **Trigger Icon**, and choose **Use Connect Flows** to pull its content from your [Connect Flows](../connect-points/setting-up-connect-flows) setup instead of filling in the bubble's fields by hand.

**Service Times** — The service times shown in your header/footer and structured data (helps Google show accurate hours in search results).

**Socials** — Links to your church's social profiles, shown as icons in your footer.

**Location** — Your church's address and map settings — also feeds your SEO structured data automatically, so filling this in helps your church show up correctly in local search and map results.

## The Game Plan

1. Start with your Brand Colors in the **Branding** tab
2. Upload your real logo and set default images
3. Pick fonts that feel like your church in **Typography**
4. Fill in **Service Times**, **Socials**, and **Location** — quick wins that also improve your SEO
5. Check the live preview on both desktop and mobile before moving on
6. Keep it simple — the best church websites aren't the flashiest, they're the clearest

:::tip
Not sure what your brand colors are? Pull them from your church logo. Most designers can give you the hex codes, or use a tool like [imagecolorpicker.com](https://imagecolorpicker.com) to sample them directly.
:::
