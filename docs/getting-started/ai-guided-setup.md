# AI Guided Setup

Starting from scratch and filling in every field by hand takes time. **AI Guided Setup** speeds that up — point it at your church's current website and it pulls your branding, colors, and content into your new FaithMade site automatically.

:::tip
This is optional. You can always set things up by hand instead — see [Setting Up Your Church Info](./setting-up-your-church-info) and [Site Settings](../design/customizing-your-sites-look).
:::

## Starting the Wizard

Click **Onboarding Wizard** in the top toolbar of your dashboard (visible on any wp-admin screen), or find it under **Site → Onboarding Wizard**.

![Welcome screen introducing Leo, the Faithmade AI, and the 5 steps it will walk through](/img/ai-onboarding/welcome.png)

Leo — the FaithMade AI — walks you through five things:

1. **Pull info from your current site** — service times, address, ministry names
2. **Build your color palette** — using colors from your existing brand
3. **Learn your church's voice** — so the copy sounds like you, not a template
4. **Rewrite the content** — with your church in mind
5. **Replace stock photos** — with real images from your site

The whole thing takes about 5 minutes. Click **Get Started**.

## Step 1: Import Your Website

![Import Your Website step with a URL field and scan consent checkbox](/img/ai-onboarding/import-website-step.png)

Enter your **current church website URL**, check the box to let FaithMade scan it for colors, logos, and sample content, and click **Start Scan**. Don't have an existing website? Click **I don't have an existing website** to skip straight to manual setup.

:::caution
Only scan a website you have the right to use — your own church's current site. The scan pulls colors, images, and text from that page to build your new site's starting point.
:::

![Scanning progress screen while Faithmade fetches your homepage](/img/ai-onboarding/scanning.png)

The scan usually takes under a minute — it's fetching your homepage and pulling out branding, colors, ministry names, and contact info.

## Step 2: Verify Church Information

![Church Information review step with editable fields for name, address, service times, and logo](/img/ai-onboarding/church-info-review.png)

Whatever the scan found gets pre-filled here — church name, address, service times, logo. **Review everything and correct anything it got wrong or missed.** This becomes your site's Church Info, the same data used in [Setting Up Your Church Info](./setting-up-your-church-info).

## Steps 3–5: Social Links, Ministry Names, and Giving

The next few steps are quick verification screens, same pattern as Church Information:

- **Social Links** — YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok URLs
- **Ministry Names** — what your church calls things: your term for sermons, kids ministry, student ministry, groups, volunteering, and membership classes
- **Giving** — your giving link or embed code, app store giving links, and text-to-give number, if you use one

## Step 6: Brand Colors

![Brand Colors step showing a large grid of colors extracted from the scanned website](/img/ai-onboarding/brand-colors.png)

Every color Leo found on your current site shows up in the **Extracted Colors** grid. Click a role under **Brand & Button Colors** (like Accent or Dark), then click a color above to assign it — the preview updates so you can see how each color looks on buttons and links before committing.

## Steps 7–9: Voice, Content, and Photos

- **Tone & Audience** — pick the tone that fits your church (warm, bold, traditional, etc.) and describe who you're speaking to. Leo uses this to write a sample of your homepage copy so you can see the voice before it rewrites your whole site.
- **Copy Preview** — review the AI-generated headline and homepage copy based on your tone and audience choices. Nothing publishes until you approve it.
- **Import Images** — swap the starter site's stock photos for real images pulled from your current website.

## Final Step: Applying Your Changes

The last step applies everything you approved — colors, content, and images — to your new site. This is the only step that actually changes your live site; everything before it is just preview and review.

:::tip
Not ready to commit? Click **Exit Guided Setup** in the top-right at any point before the final step — nothing is saved to your live site until you reach Applying.
:::
