# Moderating Prayer Requests

Every prayer request and praise report that comes in through your Prayer Wall lands in **Prayer → Prayer Requests**, where staff can review, respond to, and manage them.

---

## The Prayer Requests Queue

![Prayer Requests queue with a pending request and the Approve button highlighted](/img/prayer-wall/moderation-queue-approve.png)

If **Moderation** is set to *Require approval before publishing* in [Prayer Settings](./setting-up-the-prayer-wall), new submissions land here as **Pending** and won't appear on the public wall until you act on them. A banner at the top of the screen ("1 prayer awaiting review") lets you know when something needs attention.

Use the tabs to filter the list:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every request and praise report, any status |
| **Pending** | Awaiting your approval |
| **Approved** | Live on the public wall |
| **Private** | Submitted for staff eyes only (not shown publicly) |
| **Answered** | Marked as answered, with a response sent |
| **Archived** | Hidden from the wall but kept on record |

The **search box** filters by content or submitter name, and **Bulk action** lets you approve, delete, or archive several requests at once — check the boxes on the left, choose an action, and click **Apply**.

---

## Reviewing a Request

Click **More** on any row to expand it and see the full request text plus additional actions:

![Expanded prayer request row showing Edit content, Archive, and Change status actions](/img/prayer-wall/expanded-row-actions.png)

- **Edit content** — clean up a submission (fix a typo, remove something inappropriate) before it goes live
- **Archive** — pull it off the public wall without deleting it; archived requests can be **Restored** later
- **Change status** — jump straight to any status (Pending, Approved, Private, Answered, Archived) from the dropdown

## Row Actions

Right in the table, without expanding the row:

- **Approve** — publishes a pending request to the public wall (only shown while it's Pending)
- **Answer** — opens a compose box to email the submitter directly and mark the request Answered. If they didn't leave an email address, you can still click **Mark as Answered** to record it without sending anything.
- **Delete** — permanently removes the request

:::tip
Answering a request emails the person who submitted it — a great way to follow up personally on something your church prayed for.
:::

---

## Exporting Requests

Click **Export CSV** in the top-right corner to download every prayer request as a spreadsheet — useful for pastoral care records or reviewing trends in what your congregation is bringing to God.
