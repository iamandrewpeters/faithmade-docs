# Clearing Your Site's Cache

Your Faithmade site keeps a saved copy of each page so it loads quickly for visitors. If you've made a change and can't see it yet, that saved copy is usually why. Clearing it takes a few seconds.

## Clear your site's cache

1. Log in and visit your site.
2. In the bar across the top of the page, hover over **Cache**.
3. Choose **Purge This Page** to refresh the page you're on, or **Purge All Caches** to refresh every page on your site.

Only site administrators see the **Cache** menu. If it isn't there, ask the person who manages your Faithmade account.

To see the current state of your site's cache, choose **Cache Status** from the same menu.

## Clear your browser's cache

Your browser keeps its own copy of pages too. After purging your site's cache, reload the page without it:

- **Windows:** Ctrl + Shift + R
- **Mac:** Cmd + Shift + R
- **Phone:** close the tab and open the page again, or open it in a private window.

## Still not seeing your change?

If it still isn't showing after both, send us a request from **My requests** at the top of this page, and tick both boxes under **I have** so we know what you've already tried.
